# Flip steps only, for coach.py. See firmware/SNIFFING.md.
# Each line: how many seconds, then what to do.
# Before starting: props off, remote off, drone off.
# A flip: press the top-right button (the remote beeps), then push the
# right stick in the direction to flip. On the bench the drone won't flip.

# Switch on and pair.
8 Switch the drone on. Its light blinks slowly
8 Switch the remote on with the power switch
8 Left stick ⬆️ full forward (beep), then left stick ⬇️ full back (beep)
8 Rest: hands off. Both lights should stay on

8 Rest: hands off everything

# For comparison: the same push without the top-right button.
5 Right stick ⬆️ full forward, then let go

# One flip in each direction.
5 Top-right button 🤸: press once (it beeps), then right stick ⬆️ full forward, then let go
5 Top-right button 🤸: press once (it beeps), then right stick ➡️ full right, then let go
5 Top-right button 🤸: press once (it beeps), then right stick ⬇️ full back, then let go
5 Top-right button 🤸: press once (it beeps), then right stick ⬅️ full left, then let go

# The button alone: does anything change if the stick never moves?
8 Top-right button 🤸: press once (it beeps), and don't touch the sticks

8 Rest: hands off everything
