# Pairing steps for coach.py. See firmware/SNIFFING.md.
# Each line: how many seconds, then what to do.
# Before starting: props off, remote off, drone off.

10 Rest: remote off, drone off, hands off
30 Switch the remote on with the power switch. Hands off the sticks
5 Switch the remote off with the power switch
15 Switch the drone on. Its light blinks slowly
10 Switch the remote on with the power switch
10 Left stick ⬆️ full forward (beep), then left stick ⬇️ full back (beep)
30 Rest: hands off. Both lights should stay on
