# Control steps for coach.py. See firmware/SNIFFING.md.
# Each line: how many seconds, then what to do.
# Before starting: props off, remote and drone paired (pairing.txt).

# The baseline: what changes when nobody touches anything.
8 Rest: hands off everything

# Sticks, full: which number moves, and its two ends.
3 Left stick ⬆️ full forward
3 Left stick ⬇️ full back
3 Left stick ⬅️ full left
3 Left stick ➡️ full right
3 Right stick ⬆️ full forward
3 Right stick ⬇️ full back
3 Right stick ⬅️ full left
3 Right stick ➡️ full right

# Sticks, half: is the scale a straight line?
3 Left stick ⬆️ half forward, to the pencil mark
3 Right stick ⬆️ half forward, to the pencil mark
3 Right stick ➡️ half right, to the pencil mark

# Diagonal: are forward and right two separate numbers?
3 Right stick ↗️ full forward and full right, into the corner

# Timing: does the remote's number grow while a stick is held?
8 Right stick ⬆️ full forward, and hold it there
8 Left stick ⬆️ full forward, and hold it there
1 Right stick ⬆️ full forward, a quick tap

# Buttons.
15 Top-left button: press it again and again until it's back where it started. Count the beeps
5 Left small round button: press once
5 Left small round button: hold it down for 3 seconds
8 Left small round button: press until the drone's lights are back as they were
8 Top-right button 🤸: press once, then right stick ⬆️ full forward

# Trims: two ways to try, then undo both.
15 Trim try 1: hold the oval button down, and while holding it, push right stick ➡️ full right 3 times
15 Trim try 2: hold the oval button for 3 seconds, let go, then push right stick ➡️ full right 3 times
15 Undo try 1: hold the oval button down, and while holding it, push right stick ⬅️ full left 3 times
15 Undo try 2: hold the oval button for 3 seconds, let go, then push right stick ⬅️ full left 3 times

# Last, because they start and stop the motors. Props off!
8 Right small round button: hold it down for 3 seconds. The drone must sit level
5 Oval button: press once. The motors spin
5 Right small round button 🛑: press once. The motors stop
8 Rest: hands off everything
