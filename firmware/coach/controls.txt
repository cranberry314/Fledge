# Control steps for coach.py. See firmware/SNIFFING.md.
# Each line: how many seconds, then what to do.
# Before starting: props off, remote and drone paired (pairing.txt).

# The baseline: what changes when nobody touches anything.
10 Rest: hands off everything

# Sticks, full: which number moves, and its two ends.
3 Left stick ⬆️ full forward
3 Left stick ⬇️ full back
3 Left stick ⬅️ full left
3 Left stick ➡️ full right
3 Right stick ⬆️ full forward
3 Right stick ⬇️ full back
3 Right stick ⬅️ full left
3 Right stick ➡️ full right

# Sticks, half: is the scale a straight line?
3 Left stick ⬆️ half forward, to the pencil mark
3 Right stick ⬆️ half forward, to the pencil mark
3 Right stick ➡️ half right, to the pencil mark

# Diagonal: are forward and right two separate numbers?
3 Right stick ↗️ full forward and full right, into the corner

# Timing: does the remote's number grow while a stick is held?
10 Right stick ⬆️ full forward, and hold it there
10 Left stick ⬆️ full forward, and hold it there
1 Right stick ⬆️ full forward, a quick tap

# Buttons.
15 Button 1 (top edge, left): press it until it's back where it started. Count the beeps
5 Button 6 (front row, first round): press once
5 Button 6 (front row, first round): hold it down for 3 seconds
10 Button 6 (front row, first round): press until the lights are back as they were
10 Button 2 (top edge, right) 🤸: press once, then right stick ⬆️ full forward

# Trims: two ways to try, then undo both.
15 Trim try 1: hold button 5 (front row, left oval) down, and push right stick ➡️ full right 3 times
15 Trim try 2: hold button 5 (front row, left oval) for 3 seconds, let go, then push right stick ➡️ full right 3 times
15 Undo try 1: hold button 5 (front row, left oval) down, and push right stick ⬅️ full left 3 times
15 Undo try 2: hold button 5 (front row, left oval) for 3 seconds, let go, then push right stick ⬅️ full left 3 times

# Last, because they start and stop the motors. Props off!
8 Button 7 (front row, second round): hold it down for 3 seconds. The drone must sit level
5 Button 5 (front row, left oval): press once. The motors spin
5 Button 7 (front row, second round) 🛑: press once. The motors stop
10 Rest: hands off everything
