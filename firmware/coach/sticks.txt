# Stick sweeps only, for coach.py. See firmware/SNIFFING.md.
# Each line: how many seconds, then what to do.
# Before starting: props off, remote off, drone off.

# Switch on and pair.
8 Switch the drone on. Its light blinks slowly
8 Switch the remote on with the power switch
8 Left stick ⬆️ full forward (beep), then left stick ⬇️ full back (beep)
8 Rest: hands off. Both lights should stay on

8 Rest: hands off everything

# Every stick position, not just a few: slow circles and slow sweeps.
# Go slowly and steadily; the sniffer records about 30 positions a second.
8 Right stick: push it to the edge at ⬆️, then go slowly round clockwise 🔃, one full circle
8 Right stick: slowly from centre to ⬆️ full forward, then slowly to ⬇️ full back, then back to centre
8 Right stick: slowly from centre to ⬅️ full left, then slowly to ➡️ full right, then back to centre
8 Left stick: push it to the edge at ⬆️, then go slowly round clockwise 🔃, one full circle
8 Left stick: slowly from centre to ⬆️ full forward, then slowly to ⬇️ full back, then back to centre
8 Left stick: slowly from centre to ⬅️ full left, then slowly to ➡️ full right, then back to centre

8 Rest: hands off everything
