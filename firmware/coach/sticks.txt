# Stick steps, for coach.py. See firmware/SNIFFING.md.
# Each line: what to do. You get 15 seconds for each (see coach.py).
# Before starting: props off, remote off, drone off.

# Switch on and pair.
Switch the drone on. Its light blinks slowly
Switch the remote on with the power switch
Left stick ⬆️ full forward (beep), then left stick ⬇️ full back (beep)
Rest: hands off. Both lights should stay on

# The baseline: what changes when nobody touches anything.
Rest: hands off everything

# Each direction, full: which number moves, and its two ends.
Left stick ⬆️ full forward
Left stick ⬇️ full back
Left stick ⬅️ full left
Left stick ➡️ full right
Right stick ⬆️ full forward
Right stick ⬇️ full back
Right stick ⬅️ full left
Right stick ➡️ full right

# Half: is the scale a straight line?
Left stick ⬆️ half forward, to the pencil mark
Right stick ⬆️ half forward, to the pencil mark
Right stick ➡️ half right, to the pencil mark

# Every position: slow circles and sweeps. Go slowly and steadily; the
# sniffer records about 30 positions a second.
Right stick: push it to the edge at ⬆️, then go slowly round clockwise 🔃, one full circle
Right stick: slowly from centre to ⬆️ full forward, then slowly to ⬇️ full back, then back to centre
Right stick: slowly from centre to ⬅️ full left, then slowly to ➡️ full right, then back to centre
Left stick: push it to the edge at ⬆️, then go slowly round clockwise 🔃, one full circle
Left stick: slowly from centre to ⬆️ full forward, then slowly to ⬇️ full back, then back to centre
Left stick: slowly from centre to ⬅️ full left, then slowly to ➡️ full right, then back to centre

# Timing: does the remote's number grow while a stick is held?
Right stick ⬆️ full forward, and hold it there
Left stick ⬆️ full forward, and hold it there
Right stick ⬆️ full forward, a quick tap

Rest: hands off everything
