🃏 All the commands

calibrate()📐 Teach the drone what level is. On flat ground, before take_off().
take_off()🛫 Take off and hover
land()🛬 Come down and stop
up(1) down(1)⬆️⬇️ Go up or down for 1 second
forward(1) back(1)⏩⏪ Fly forward or back for 1 second
left(1) right(1)⬅️➡️ Slide sideways for 1 second
rotate(90)🔄 Spin right a quarter turn. rotate(-90) spins left, rotate(360) all the way round.
wait(1)⏸️ Hover still for 1 second
repeat 4:🔁 Do the lines under it 4 times. Put 4 spaces in front of each one.
# a note💬 After a # is a note for people. The drone skips it.

📏 The rules

  • ⏱️ A program lasts seconds at most. Then the drone lands by itself.
  • 📦 The drone stays within seconds of flying from where it took off: ahead, behind, left and right. Ahead is the way it faces at the start.
  • ⬆️ It goes at most seconds of up() above where it hovers.
  • ✅ The program is checked before the drone takes off, so a mistake never stops it in the air.
